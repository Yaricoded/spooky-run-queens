Island only

Skip Calvary Cemetery. Run the first three stops, which are all on Roosevelt Island, then finish at the Roosevelt Island Bridge or the F train and tram. That is about 2.9 miles including the run back south from the lighthouse, and every stop on it is outdoors.

Add-on: Steinway Mansion, Astoria

18-33 41st St · private, view from the street

This 27-room summer house was built in 1857–58 by Benjamin Pike Jr., and the piano maker William Steinway bought it in 1870. It is a New York City Landmark. I could not find a documented ghost story or regular public hours for it, and it is about 4 miles northeast of the Roosevelt Island Bridge, so it is not one of the stops.

Notes. Leg distances and times are estimates, not GPS measurements, and the long leg to Calvary crosses the Roosevelt Island Bridge, so check the live directions in Google Maps. Fright levels are our own rating. The Smallpox Hospital ruin is fenced off for now and is seen from outside. The Octagon is a private apartment building, so the run views it from the sidewalk. The story of the carved stone at the lighthouse is a legend. I did not find a documented ghost story for the other stops, so their fright levels reflect the history rather than reported hauntings.
